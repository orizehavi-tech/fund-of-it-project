# AI Assistance Disclosure

We used ChatGPT (OpenAI) as a supporting tool while working on our Northern Lights project.

After receiving feedback on our original proposal, we consulted ChatGPT about possible ways to use the NOAA and GFZ Kp data. We reviewed the available fields and considered how the forecasts could be compared with measured geomagnetic activity. We decided to focus the project on the accuracy of NOAA and GFZ Kp forecasts.

ChatGPT also assisted with the technical preparation of our data collection and with organizing the three source responses (NOAA forecast, GFZ forecast, and GFZ reported Kp) into one timestamped JSON snapshot per collection. We ran the collections ourselves on our Linux virtual machine and reviewed the saved files. All forecast and observation values came from the original data providers, not from AI-generated estimates.

For the documentation, we shared the assignment instructions and our project information with ChatGPT and asked it to arrange the content in Markdown. This included the required sections of `SOURCE.md`.

We are responsible for the final project choices and for checking the collected data and documentation. Any analysis of forecast accuracy will be based on the data we collected.
